Refunds

  • Within 14 days of any charge — the first payment or a renewal — ask and it is refunded in full. No reason is needed.
  • A refunded subscription ends at once. A refunded one-time purchase takes back what it added.
  • After 14 days a charge is not refunded, except where the law where you live requires it. You keep what you paid for until the end of its period.
  • To ask: write to magenie33@moginlabs.com from the address on your receipt, or use Link's support from the receipt email. Link may also refund a payment itself within 60 days.
  • The refund goes back to the way you paid; your bank decides how long it takes to show.
