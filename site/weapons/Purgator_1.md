## Not modelled here

- 'Enemies directly killed by Purgator 1 leave behind 1 Gas status effect stack', and 'the gas cloud deals damage each tick equal to HALF of the killing shot's damage' (wiki). It is an Extra Hit keyed on a KILL rather than on a hit, it takes the killing shot's crit tier and faction bonuses and no multishot, and this engine has no shape for a hit that only exists once something has died. Against anything that dies, this weapon is understated by roughly a third of its real output
- INFINITE BODY PUNCH THROUGH: the page grants this weapon 1.1 m of punch through AND unlimited penetration of BODIES specifically. The 1.1 m is transcribed and spends `space::BODY_MATERIAL_M` a body like every other weapon's, so the slug stops after two enemies here where in game it would cross the whole line
