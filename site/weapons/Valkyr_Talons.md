# Valkyr Talons

Chinese name: Valkyr 的利爪

Claws · Exalted · Mastery Rank 0. 250 base damage (puncture 62.5, slash 187.5), 50% crit chance, 2x crit multiplier, 10% status chance.

## Not modelled here

- Hysteria drains 5 energy a second while the claws are out; energy is not modelled, so they are held for the whole engagement
- every hit restores 100 health, and nothing in this arena damages the Tenno
- one attack input's own animation length is not published anywhere: the wiki gives a combo's total duration at 1.0x attack speed and one entry per input, so the combo's DURATION is exact and the split between its INPUTS is even. It moves a status tick's start by fractions of a second inside a combo and moves no total
