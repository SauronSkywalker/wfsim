# Sigma & Octantis

Chinese name: 西格玛 & 南极座

Sword And Shield · Melee · Mastery Rank 10. 174 base damage (impact 38.28, puncture 27.84, slash 107.88), 28% crit chance, 2.2x crit multiplier, 16% status chance.

## Not modelled here

- one attack input's own animation length is not published anywhere: the wiki gives a combo's total duration at 1.0x attack speed and one entry per input, so the combo's DURATION is exact and the split between its INPUTS is even. It moves a status tick's start by fractions of a second inside a combo and moves no total
- Power Spike's partial combo decay is a Warframe passive and is not modelled: this counter drops to zero when its clock runs out, so a build running that passive keeps far more of it than this reports
- not modelled — Shield Throw and Shield Throw Explosion: of this weapon's attacks only the swing, the slam and the heavy slam are
- the wiki's stance tables give this weapon combos of its OWN (aerial attack), which replace the equipped stance's and are not transcribed: the stance's (or its class's) are played in their place
- the wiki publishes no slide attack figure or no slide duration for this weapon, so it has no slide mode here
- not modelled — each blocked attack stores a charge of +15% Critical Chance and +15% Status Chance for the next attack, up to 4
