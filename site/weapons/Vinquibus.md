## Not modelled here

- the second INNATE POLARITY the wiki publishes for this weapon is an AURA one, and no weapon mod carries an Aura polarity — so this entry gives that slot no colour at all, which costs a build the discount a matching slot would have paid. Capacity here is therefore a FLOOR: whatever that slot does in game, it cannot be worse than unpolarised
- EQUIPPING THIS WEAPON OCCUPIES THE MELEE SLOT TOO, so a Vinquibus loadout gives up a melee weapon entirely — a cost this arena cannot price, because it has no melee to give up
- headshots grant 2 stacks (max 16) of 'Vinquibus Precision', increasing MELEE damage by 250% — the buff is real and lands on a weapon this sim does not fire, so it is worth nothing here and a great deal to the player it was designed for
