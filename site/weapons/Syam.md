# Syam

Chinese name: 业珀

Nikana · Melee · Mastery Rank 0. 270 base damage (impact 54, puncture 108, slash 108), 22% crit chance, 2x crit multiplier, 34% status chance.

## Not modelled here

- One attack input's animation length is unpublished: the wiki gives each combo's total at 1.0x attack speed, so totals are exact and inputs are split evenly. This shifts a status tick by fractions of a second and changes no total.
- Power Spike's partial combo decay is a Warframe passive and is not modelled: the counter drops to zero when its timer runs out, so with that passive the number reads low.
- Not modelled: First Shockwave and Second Shockwave. Of this weapon's attacks, only the swing, the slam and the heavy slam are.
- This weapon's heavy attack is two shockwaves that travel 230 m and pierce: 500 Heat with a forced Impact status, then 1500 Heat, which is not modelled, so there is no heavy mode and a Tennokai window converts nothing.
