## Not modelled here

- the THREE BOMBLETS the sac splits into are not fired. The module lists a 'Bomblet Impact' and a 2.1 m 'Bomblet Explosion' (Reduction 0.8, so falloff to 20%), both Toxin; the CO catalog prices a Bomblet Impact at 9 on a grip it does not name, and nothing prices the Bomblet Explosion. The whole of the chamber's Toxin goes to the first explosion, which over-pays it if the bomblets share that Toxin and under-pays the shot if they add to it
- the CO catalog's row is 'Sporelacer (Secondary) | Normal Attack | 90.5 | 57 | 63% | Adding | Some reason CO-bonus uses 57 damage value for any configuration' — a term on a FIXED 57 whatever the grip, which no fraction of the direct hit can say. The direct hit takes the ordinary term on its own Impact instead: none at all on the default Gibber grip, 261 on Haymaker
- the projectile falls off from 13 m to 26 m to an amount nobody publishes ('100% to ?%' on the page; the module's author marks its own figure unknown), so no falloff is applied
- the sac ARCS heavily, 'reduced by Projectile Speed'
- the projectile has TRAVEL TIME and has to be led — 'hitting a target at range requires leading the target before firing'. Every shot here connects the instant it is fired, so this weapon's real hit rate against anything that moves is worse than the numbers below
- the blast staggers the WIELDER in game — this arena gives the player no body, so a build that would be unplayable in a corridor costs nothing here
