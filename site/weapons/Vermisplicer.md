# Vermisplicer

Chinese name: 虫置

Rifle · Primary · Mastery Rank 0. 33 base damage (impact 5, puncture 6, slash 10, toxin 12), 25% crit chance, 2x crit multiplier, 25% status chance.

## Not modelled here

- the three tendrils FAN OUT from the struck enemy ('within 6 meters of the main target'), where the chain here is a path whose every hop measures 6 m from the last. Against a tight group the two agree; a third enemy 6-12 m from the first is reached here and not in game
- with PUNCH THROUGH the chains come off 'the furthest target hit by the primary beam that can spawn the chain beams', and only one target spawns them; here every body the beam crosses seeds its own
- 'Chain beams will not target an enemy that has already been hit by the primary beam'; here a chain may strike a body the beam punched through
- the tendril auto-targets, which leaves 'a noticeable delay between opening fire and actually dealing damage', ammo spent all the while
