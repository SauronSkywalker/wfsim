## Not modelled here

- the projectile is a WALL 3 metres thick, which with the infinite body punch through above is most of what this weapon is in a room: one shot sweeps everything in a 3 m band. This arena fires a RAY, so only the bodies on the line are struck and the width buys nothing. The Tenet variant's sheet is 4.4 m
- the guaranteed Impact proc is capped at 14 metres and is applied at every distance here, since a forced proc carries no range of its own. It overstates the shot past 14 m, where the damage is already most of the way to its falloff floor
- the innate punch through does not apply to SURFACES when the central portion of the projectile hits one, which the page says makes punch-through MODS worth fitting anyway — a geometry rule in an arena with no geometry
