# Zenistar

Chinese name: 天顶之星

Heavy Blade · Melee · Mastery Rank 6. 298 base damage (heat 150, impact 80, slash 68), 10% crit chance, 2x crit multiplier, 30% status chance.

## Not modelled here

- one attack input's own animation length is not published anywhere: the wiki gives a combo's total duration at 1.0x attack speed and one entry per input, so the combo's DURATION is exact and the split between its INPUTS is even. It moves a status tick's start by fractions of a second inside a combo and moves no total
- Power Spike's partial combo decay is a Warframe passive and is not modelled: this counter drops to zero when its clock runs out, so a build running that passive keeps far more of it than this reports
- not modelled — Attacks While Disc Deployed: of this weapon's attacks only the swing, the slam and the heavy slam are
- this weapon's heavy attack is a thrown disc: 75 Impact on contact, a 350 Heat burst, then a 4 m aura of 50 Heat every 0.8 s for 10 s, longer with the combo multiplier, which is not modelled, so it has no heavy mode here and a Tennokai window converts nothing
