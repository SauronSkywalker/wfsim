# Riot-848

Chinese name: 暴乱-848

Pistol · Secondary · Mastery Rank 14. 12 base damage (puncture 12), 26% crit chance, 2.2x crit multiplier, 26% status chance.

## Not modelled here

- THE EXPLOSION IS A RELOAD, not an impact: 'bullets lodge into surfaces. Upon reloading, the bullets explode in a 1.5 meter radius before flying back to the wielder', up to 32 at a time (wiki). This engine's explosions go off where the shot lands, which would fire it on every hit and at the wrong time — so the 48 Blast a round is NOT counted at all, and against anything that survives a magazine this weapon is understated by roughly four fifths of its real output
- it 'does not use ammo pickups' — the module gives it a reserve of zero, and this sim assumes infinite reserves anyway, so the constraint the weapon is built around does not exist here
