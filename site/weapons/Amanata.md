# Amanata

Chinese name: 天薙刀

Polearm · Melee · Mastery Rank 2. 126 base damage (puncture 37.8, slash 88.2), 24% crit chance, 2x crit multiplier, 24% status chance.

## Not modelled here

- One attack input's animation length is unpublished: the wiki gives each combo's total at 1.0x attack speed, so totals are exact and inputs are split evenly. This shifts a status tick by fractions of a second and changes no total.
- Power Spike's partial combo decay is a Warframe passive and is not modelled: the counter drops to zero when its timer runs out, so with that passive the number reads low.
- The wiki publishes no heavy attack figure for this weapon, so there is no heavy mode.
- Not modelled: Amanata's Rolls: a random blessing — 40% final Critical Chance, 30% final Status Chance, 30% Status Damage, +1 m range, 20% attack speed, or all five — rerolled every 30 hits.
