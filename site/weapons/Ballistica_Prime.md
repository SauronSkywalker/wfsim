## Not modelled here

- a PARTLY charged shot is not modelled — releasing early multiplies the Normal Shot's OWN base by (1 + charge progress) and adds the evolution's flat base damage beside that multiplier rather than inside it (M79), so a 50% charge is 40 x 1.5 + 3 rather than 43 x 1.5; this entry carries only the two ends of that ramp: no charge at all, and the full charge that fires this attack instead
- a GHOST is counted, not fought: a charged-shot kill leaves one for 7 seconds and the run reports how many stood, but what a ghost does is not modelled — it neither attacks nor blocks a shot of yours, so nothing in the fight reads it back
